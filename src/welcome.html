PopShield is on

Ad tabs, popunders and redirect hijacks are now blocked. Tabs you open yourself keep working.

✕ Blocked

Ad tabs that open when you click a video player or anywhere on a page, popunders, invisible ad layers, and tricks that send your tab to an ad.

✓ Still works

Links you click, ctrl- and middle-click, “Open link in new tab”, share and sign-in buttons, downloads. A button that waits a moment before opening a window works for the same site and for sign-in and payment pages.

How it decides

A site may open a new tab only when your click was really meant for the thing you clicked — not for a hidden ad hook on the whole page.

Pin PopShield to your toolbar

  1. Click the puzzle-piece Extensions icon at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Click the pin next to PopShield.
  3. The badge on the shield shows how many things were blocked on the current page. Click it to see the list, open anything you actually wanted, or report a site.

Three modes, per site

Smart (default)
Blocks what ad scripts trigger; tabs you open yourself still work.
Strict
No script may open a tab at all. Only links you click yourself can.
Off
PopShield does nothing on that site.

Your privacy

PopShield never records which sites you visit. It only counts how many things it blocked — a number, kept on this device. The list of what was blocked on a page lives only in that tab and disappears when you leave the page or close the tab.

No telemetry, no tracking, no network requests. The Report buttons only open a GitHub form; nothing is sent unless you choose to submit it.
