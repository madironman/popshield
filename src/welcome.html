PopShield is on

Ad tabs, popunders and redirect hijacks are now being blocked. Tabs you open yourself keep working.

✕ Blocked

Ad tabs that open when you click a button or anywhere on a page, popunders, invisible ad layers, and tricks that send your tab to an ad.

✓ Still works

Links you click, ctrl- and middle-click, “Open link in new tab”, share and sign-in buttons, downloads. A button that waits a moment before opening a window works for the same site and for sign-in and payment pages.

How it decides

A site may open a new tab only when your click was really meant for the thing you clicked — not for a hidden ad hook on the whole page.

Pin PopShield to your toolbar

  1. Click the puzzle-piece Extensions icon at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Click the pin next to PopShield.
  3. The badge on the shield shows how many things were blocked on the current page. Click it to see the list, open anything you actually wanted, or report a site.

Three modes, per site

Smart (default)
Blocks what ad scripts trigger; tabs you open yourself still work, and so do payment pages that redirect you to your bank a few seconds after you click.
Strict
Maximum protection: no script may open a tab, and once you've used a page no script may send it to another site. Only links you click yourself can.
Off
PopShield does nothing on that site.

Your privacy

PopShield keeps no browsing history. The only site names it saves are the ones you choose a mode for; everything else is an anonymous count. The list of what was blocked on a page lives only in that tab and is cleared when you go to a new page or close the tab.

No telemetry, no tracking, no remote code. If you press a Report button, PopShield first shows what will be shared, then opens github.com with those details filled in: the page’s address (without the part after ? or #), your mode, version numbers and what was blocked. GitHub receives them when the tab opens; nothing becomes public unless you sign in and submit. The Report buttons are hidden in incognito windows.
